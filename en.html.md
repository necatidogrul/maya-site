# Maya — AI chef app: recipes from what's in your fridge (iPhone)

> Maya is a pocket AI chef for iPhone. Photograph your fridge, type or say what you have, and in
> about 30 seconds she writes two recipes built around the tastes, allergies and diet she has
> learned from you. At the hour you choose she brings one dinner decision before you ask. Cook
> hands-free step by step, ask about swaps while cooking, scan a plate for a calorie estimate.

## How it works

1. Show or tell: fridge photo, typed ingredients, or voice.
2. Two personal recipes in about 30 seconds, with photos and steps.
3. Hands-free cooking mode; chat about swaps and adjustments.

## More than recipes

- Evening decision at your chosen hour.
- Learns tastes, allergies, diet and what you cooked.
- Plate scan: calorie and macro estimate with a confidence level.
- Turkish home cooking and global dishes.

## Questions

**What is Maya?** An AI chef for iPhone. Photograph your fridge, type or say what you have, and Maya writes two recipes for you in about 30 seconds, shaped by the tastes, allergies and diet it has learned from you. It also brings you one dinner decision at the hour you choose, so you never have to ask "what's for dinner" again.

**How is it different from a recipe app?** A recipe app searches recipes that already exist. Maya writes one from what you actually have. It remembers what you liked, cooks with you hands-free step by step, and answers questions like "what can I swap for onion?" while you cook.

**Is it free?** Free to download, with one evening decision and two free recipes every day, voice search and plate calorie scan. Maya Premium unlocks unlimited recipes, unlimited chat and advanced nutrition filters. The weekly plan starts with a 3-day free trial; monthly and yearly plans are also available. Prices are shown in the app before purchase.

**Does it know Turkish cooking?** Yes. Maya was built in Turkey and covers Turkish home cooking as well as global dishes. The interface is available in English and Turkish.

**How accurate is the plate calorie scan?** It is an estimate from a photo, based on recognising the dish and guessing the portion, and it shows its confidence level. It is not medical or dietary advice.

**Which phones?** iPhone running iOS 16 or later. There is no Android version.
