## Three steps, sixty seconds

No account, no sign-up. Your first plan is built before you finish onboarding.

1. **Tell it** — How many nights you really cook, how many are at the table, what to keep off
   your plate. Most people plan seven and cook four; Maya plans the real number.
2. **Get the plan** — One dinner per day, with cook time, servings and the full recipe. Swap
   anything you don't want, leave a night for leftovers.
3. **Take the list** — The shopping list comes out of the plan by itself: produce, meat, dairy,
   pantry, spices, each its own heading.

## You buy the lemons once

If two meals in your plan call for lemons, the list shows one line and the quantities add up.
Swap a meal and the list recalculates: what left comes off, what arrived goes on, and anything
shared merges again.

It sounds like a detail. Of the 37 recipe and meal-planning apps reviewed on the Turkish and US
stores, four did it.

## What's inside

- **Weekly meal plan** — You choose how many nights. One dinner per day, any night left empty,
  any meal swapped.
- **Shopping list** — Sorted by aisle, quantities merged. Add your own items, share the list with
  whoever shops, tick things off in the store.
- **Portion scaling** — Two servings to six in a single tap; recipe amounts and list quantities
  move together.
- **385-dish catalogue** — 265 Turkish classics and 120 dishes from around the world, every one
  with the full recipe, free. Filter by time, ingredient and method (oven, air fryer, one-pot).
- **Hands-free cook mode** — The screen stays awake; tap a time in the recipe and a countdown
  starts.
- **Type it, say it, or photograph the fridge** — Three ways in, all ending in the same plan. The
  fridge photo is a feature, not the headline.
- **An AI that remembers you** — Keeps your tastes, allergies and the foods you'd rather skip.
  Ask about a recipe while you cook: "What can I swap for onion?"
- **One message a day** — With permission, tonight's dinner from your own plan, plus one
  shopping-day reminder.

## Questions

**How is this different from a recipe app?**
A recipe app gives you somewhere to search. Maya decides: it fills a week with dinners you'd
actually cook and turns that week into one grocery list. The recipes are there — 385 of them,
free — but the product is the plan and the list.

**How does the shopping list work?**
It is built from your plan and sorted by aisle: produce, meat, dairy, pantry, spices. Repeated
ingredients merge into one line with the quantities added up. You can add your own items and
share the list.

**Is the 385-dish catalogue really free?**
Yes — all of it, with full recipes: 265 Turkish classics and 120 dishes from around the world.
What the free tier limits is AI plan generation, not the catalogue.

**Can I still photograph my fridge?**
Yes. Typing, speaking and photographing all work. The photo is one of three ways into a plan.

**Is there a free trial?**
A free trial is offered on the weekly plan only and lasts 3 days; the monthly and annual plans
have no trial. Maya Pro unlocks unlimited weekly plans, unlimited shopping lists and the whole
catalogue. Prices are shown before purchase.

**Where does my data go?**
Your plan, list, favourites and preferences stay on the device. Only the ingredients you type,
your preferences and — if you use it — the photo you take are sent to the AI service that writes
the recipe; photos are processed and not stored. No advertising SDK, no tracking SDK, no IDFA.

**Which devices?**
iPhone running iOS 16 or later. There is no Android version. The interface is in English and
Turkish; the App Store listing is in 21 languages.
