Three steps, sixty seconds

No account, no sign-up. Your first plan is built before you finish onboarding.

  1. Tell it

    How many nights you really cook, how many are at the table, what to keep off your plate. Most people plan seven and cook four — Maya plans the real number.

  2. Get the plan

    One dinner per day, with cook time, servings and the full recipe. Swap anything you don't want, leave a night for leftovers.

  3. Take the list

    The shopping list comes out of the plan by itself: produce, meat, dairy, pantry, spices, each its own heading.

You buy the lemons once.

If two meals in your plan call for lemons, the list shows one line and the quantities add up. Swap a meal and the list recalculates: what left comes off, what arrived goes on, and anything shared merges again.

Questions

How is this different from a recipe app?

A recipe app gives you somewhere to search. Maya decides: it fills a week with dinners you'd actually cook and turns that week into one grocery list. The recipes are there — 385 of them, free — but the product is the plan and the list.

How does the shopping list work?

It is built from your plan and sorted by aisle: produce, meat, dairy, pantry, spices. Repeated ingredients merge into one line with the quantities added up. You can add your own items and share the list.

Is the 385-dish catalogue really free?

Yes — all of it, with full recipes. 265 Turkish classics and 120 dishes from around the world. What the free tier limits is AI plan generation, not the catalogue.

Can I still photograph my fridge?

Yes. Typing, speaking and photographing all work. The photo is one of three ways into a plan, not the headline.

Is there a free trial?

A free trial is offered on the weekly plan only and lasts 3 days; the monthly and annual plans have no trial. Maya Pro unlocks unlimited weekly plans, unlimited shopping lists and the whole catalogue. Prices are shown before purchase — see the Terms of Use.

Where does my data go?

Your plan, your list, your favourites and your preferences stay on the device. Only the ingredients you type, your preferences and — if you use it — the photo you take are sent to the AI service that writes the recipe; photos are processed and not stored. No advertising SDK, no tracking SDK, no IDFA. See the Privacy Policy.

Which devices?

iPhone running iOS 16 or later. There is no Android version. The interface is in English and Turkish; the App Store listing is in 21 languages.
