What's for dinner?
Ask the fridge.

Maya is a pocket AI chef. Photograph your fridge, type or say what you have, and in about 30 seconds she writes two recipes built around your taste. Not a cookbook. A chef who remembers you.

How it works

  1. Show or tell. Snap your fridge, type ingredients, or say "I have chicken, peppers and half a pack of pasta".
  2. Maya thinks. Two personal recipes in about 30 seconds, with photos and steps, shaped by what you liked before.
  3. Cook and ask. Hands-free step-by-step mode. Stuck? "What can I swap for onion?" "Can we make this lighter?"

More than recipes

  • The evening decision. At the hour you choose, Maya brings one dinner idea before you ask.
  • She learns you. Tastes, allergies and diet are remembered; suggestions get closer every day.
  • Plate scan. Photograph your plate and get a calorie and macro estimate, with a confidence level.
  • Turkish home cooking and global dishes. Built in Turkey; interface in English and Turkish.

Pricing

Free to download. Every day you get one evening decision and two free recipes, plus voice search and plate scan. Maya Premium unlocks unlimited recipes, unlimited chat and advanced nutrition filters. The weekly plan starts with a 3-day free trial; monthly and yearly plans are also available. Prices are shown in the app before purchase; cancel any time from your Apple ID settings.

Questions

What is Maya?

An AI chef for iPhone. Photograph your fridge, type or say what you have, and Maya writes two recipes for you in about 30 seconds, shaped by the tastes, allergies and diet it has learned from you. It also brings you one dinner decision at the hour you choose, so you never have to ask "what's for dinner" again.

How is it different from a recipe app?

A recipe app searches recipes that already exist. Maya writes one from what you actually have. It remembers what you liked, cooks with you hands-free step by step, and answers questions like "what can I swap for onion?" while you cook.

Is it free?

Free to download, with one evening decision and two free recipes every day, voice search and plate calorie scan. Maya Premium unlocks unlimited recipes, unlimited chat and advanced nutrition filters. The weekly plan starts with a 3-day free trial; monthly and yearly plans are also available. Prices are shown in the app before purchase.

Does it know Turkish cooking?

Yes. Maya was built in Turkey and covers Turkish home cooking as well as global dishes. The interface is available in English and Turkish.

How accurate is the plate calorie scan?

It is an estimate from a photo, based on recognising the dish and guessing the portion, and it shows its confidence level. It is not medical or dietary advice.

Which phones?

iPhone running iOS 16 or later. There is no Android version.
